cd swarmkit-market && npm ci
SWARMKIT_EXCHANGE=exchange… \
claude mcp add swarmkit -- node mcp/server.mjs

Every allocation line must match the contract's, no buyer may pay above its escrow, no provider may sell past its revealed capacity, and the contract must have no access keys.

      Mainnet Proof of Round and NEAR Intents

      read from mainnet now

      A 478-byte anchor contract, written byte by byte, holds a mainnet round's record: each seller anchored its sealed bid from its own account before the coordinator anchored "sealed", so no bid could be added or dropped later. Four buyer-signed payments then settled through execute_intents on intents.near.
