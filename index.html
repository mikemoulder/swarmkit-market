What every agent decided

    What runs on NEAR

    One account per agent

    Your team’s agent and each provider’s agent have their own NEAR testnet sub-account, so every payment traces to one agent. The other 19 teams are simulated.

    Sealed bids, checked

    Providers commit a SHA-256 hash first. A reveal that does not match its hash is rejected.

    Pay only your share

    The SDK turns the allocation into one payment per team and provider. Press Settle and your share is paid on NEAR testnet through the SDK’s crash-safe runner; a retry never pays twice. The same runner has settled through NEAR Intents on mainnet.
